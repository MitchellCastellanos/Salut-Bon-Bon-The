# Marketing plan

Fresh Mango Series leads the commercial homepage. Crème Brûlée, House Lemonade and Explore Our Menu have bilingual landing pages linked from the homepage. The first three use official product associations and the menu campaign links to four core categories. All remain catalog previews while approved checkout is disabled.

Keep Récompenses Salut / Salut Rewards in preview until program rules and account flows are approved. Use the existing brand palette and [image manifest](IMAGE_ASSET_MANIFEST.md). Review every asset against the recipe and verify mobile crops before publishing.

The import seeds the authorized campaigns active with no scheduled end. The ADMIN console controls publication, activation, homepage position and display dates. Collections remain independently browsable when campaigns leave the homepage.

Optional first-party analytics is off by default. It records event names, campaign IDs when applicable, and timestamps for opted-in browsers. Counts are approximate and may include repeated visits. Review consent language, retention and removal policy before enabling analytics in a public deployment.
