# Demo walkthrough

1. Run the local environment using the [setup guide](ENVIRONMENT_SETUP.md), leaving Neon and staff credentials unset.
2. Open the French homepage, use EN and browse the menu. Customize a mango tea with large size, half sugar, no ice and popping pearls.
3. Add it to the cart, choose a pickup time and submit under a fictitious name. Choose pay at pickup, or use the explicitly labeled simulated method for a second order.
4. Copy the tracking link. Open the staff area separately, sign in with the documented demo password, then confirm, prepare and mark the order ready.
5. Return to tracking and observe the update after the next refresh. For pay at pickup, record payment in the staff area and complete the order. Tracking should show completed.
6. Restart the app and revisit the link to confirm persistence. Loyalty, real communications and analytics demos are pending.
