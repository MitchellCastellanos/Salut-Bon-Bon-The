# Premium roadmap

- Sales analytics and exports depend on settled payment and refund records. Add period aggregates and retention reporting.
- Referral campaigns need codes, attribution, first purchase qualification and abuse controls.
- Marketing automation needs campaign schedules, promotion rules, segmentation, consent and delivery reports.
- Gift cards need issuance, unique codes, a transactional balance ledger and refund and partial redemption rules.
- Apple Wallet loyalty requires pass signing, a developer account or paid provider, updates and certificate renewal.
