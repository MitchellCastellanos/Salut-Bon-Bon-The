# Owner approval checklist

- [ ] Confirm base, size, variant and modifier prices in CAD and the tax treatment.
- [ ] Confirm sizes, eligible modifiers, default selections and availability per product.
- [ ] Confirm store address, hours, lead time, per-slot capacity and official social profile.
- [ ] Approve campaign copy, photography, brand assets and any promotion terms.
- [ ] Approve loyalty rules, privacy disclosures, payment terms and notification consent. For Salut Rewards specifically (see [proposal](LOYALTY_COMMERCIAL_PROPOSAL.md)): confirm 10 points per $1 and 1,000 points = $5, eligible categories, minimum purchase, expiry (none today), promotion stacking, the accountant's view on the discount, the commercial-authorization checkbox and launch date, and whether the discount should one day apply automatically online (not built).
- [ ] Sign off after preview and production smoke tests.
