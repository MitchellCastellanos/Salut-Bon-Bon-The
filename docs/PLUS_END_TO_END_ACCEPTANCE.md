# Salut Plus — end-to-end client demonstration acceptance contract
Date: 2026-11-20. Status: TARGET SPECIFICATION, NOT VERIFIED DELIVERY.

## Goal
Demonstrate a genuine integrated customer journey in FR and EN: register -> verify email -> browse official priced catalog -> customize drink -> cart -> order -> Stripe TEST payment -> staff processing -> eligible order completed -> one stamp per eligible paid drink -> customer rewards card with official centered logo QR -> add to Apple Wallet -> staff scan identifies customer -> staff award/redeem with audit. Never claim real payments or production readiness based on test mode.

## Definition of done
1. Customer creates account, verifies email, signs in/out, recovers password; customer and staff roles remain isolated. Test with configured Resend sandbox and disposable test identities.
2. Official catalog, actual configured prices, included tapioca and +$1 per additional approved topping (unless current owner-approved rules differ), size upgrade, taxes and modifiers calculated on server; verify with official price sheet.
3. Cart, checkout, idempotent order creation, Stripe test-mode checkout and webhook reconciliation. No fake HTML credit card collection; demonstrate Stripe-hosted test UI. Customer sees accurate order history/status; staff can move order through lifecycle. Failed/retried payments cannot duplicate orders or stamps.
4. Reward scheme is **9 stamps then 10th eligible drink free**, NOT historical 5% points. Award on verified completion only, no duplicates; test cancellations/refunds, eligible categories and thresholds. Redemption happens at counter unless separately approved for online checkout. Keep loyalty inactive in production pending owner authorization.
5. On customer account and guest card where applicable, show readable branded QR with official logo centered as large as practical while retaining scan reliability. Use QR error correction H, adequate quiet zone, high contrast, safe logo backing, no stretching, responsive sizes; measure scan success with iPhone and Android cameras at multiple distances and brightness levels. Do not promise a fixed maximum logo percentage; tune based on tests.
6. Apple Wallet: genuine signed .pkpass (pass type and team certificates, Apple Developer prerequisites), Add to Apple Wallet action, correct branding, customer identity and stamp progress; test install and barcode scan on real iPhone. No screenshots pretending to be Wallet. Ensure wallet pass can be revoked/updated according to a documented lifecycle; credentials stay server-side and out of git.
7. **Security architecture decision:** current 5-minute single-use QR URL is for sensitive redemption and MUST NOT be embedded as a permanent Wallet barcode. Implement separate opaque, stable, revocable **identification** token for Wallet/printed QR; staff authentication is required for lookup. Staff actions (award/redeem) require a fresh authenticated confirmation, idempotency and audit; no customer PII in barcode. Design for leaked-token mitigation, rotation and rate limits. Keep short-lived redemption QR where appropriate.
8. Staff scanner works with phone camera or external scanner/manual input; requires staff role. Test denied anonymous and customer attempts; ensure repeat scans never award twice.
9. Bilingual UX, accessibility, mobile-first, empty/loading/error states and privacy consent verified. No real customer personal data in demo.
10. Run checks, unit, integration, real PostgreSQL concurrency, browser E2E and production build. Validate on Vercel preview with isolated Neon branch, test Stripe and sandbox email; record evidence, failing cases and remaining external prerequisites.
